Our Story

About Elite Prospects Hockey

The premier invite-only showcase for elite youth hockey players.

Our Mission

Identifying the Next Generation of Stars

Elite Prospects Hockey was built on a simple belief: the best young players deserve a stage worthy of their talent. Founded by former players and scouts who understood what elite youth hockey exposure truly means, we created a showcase experience unlike anything else in the game.

Every aspect of our event — from the rink conditions to the guest evaluators — is designed to give players the best possible platform to be seen, evaluated, and advanced to the next level of their careers.

Quality Over Quantity

We strictly limit roster spots to maintain the elite standard our scouts expect and our players deserve.

Maximum Exposure

We partner with scouts, coaches, and organizations from AAA, USHL, OHL, and NCAA programs at every event.

Development Focused

Beyond evaluation, our events include on-ice coaching sessions led by professional and collegiate level coaches.

Our Journey

How We Got Here

2018

The First Showcase

Elite Prospects Hockey held its inaugural showcase event, featuring standout players from the 2014 and 2015 birth year classes. Twenty-two elite scouts attended. The standard was set from day one.

2019

Expansion to 5 Birth Years

Based on overwhelming demand from the youth hockey community, we expanded our showcase program to include all five birth years (2014–2018), running dedicated events for each class.

2021

Alumni Begin Advancing

Our first wave of showcase alumni began committing to AAA programs and junior leagues. The results validated everything we built — quality evaluation leads to quality outcomes.

2023

Nationwide Scout Network

Elite Prospects Hockey formalized partnerships with scouting staffs across major junior programs, creating a direct pipeline from our showcase floor to elite development leagues.

2025

The Standard Continues

With over 300 players evaluated and alumni spread across top leagues nationwide, Elite Prospects Hockey continues to be the most respected youth hockey showcase in the region. Who's Got Next?
