Who We Are

The Premier Youth Hockey Showcase

Elite Prospects Hockey was founded with one mission: to identify, develop, and expose the very best youth hockey talent in North America. Our invite-only showcase events bring together elite-level players from each birth year in a high-competition environment watched by college scouts, junior programs, and top development coaches.

Our alumni have gone on to play at the highest levels — AAA, USHL, OHL, NCAA — and our reputation is built entirely on the quality of talent we showcase.

What We Offer

Built for Elite Competition

Scout Exposure

Top-tier scouts from AAA programs, junior leagues, and NCAA institutions attend every showcase event to evaluate talent firsthand.

Invite Only

Every player must be submitted and evaluated. Only those meeting elite standards receive an invitation, ensuring the highest level of competition.

Birth Year Classes

We host separate showcase divisions for each birth year (2014–2018), ensuring players compete against true age-group peers.

Proven Alumni

Our alumni are playing at every level of the game. The Elite Prospects track record is our strongest recruiting tool — results speak for themselves.
