FAQ

Common Questions

How do I submit a player?

Use the Player Submission Form on our homepage. Fill out all required fields including position, birth year, and contact info. Video highlights are strongly encouraged.

Is the showcase invite-only?

Yes. All participants must receive an official invitation from Elite Prospects Hockey staff following evaluation of their submission.

What birth years are eligible?

We currently run showcase events for players born in 2014, 2015, 2016, 2017, and 2018. Each birth year has its own dedicated event.

Who attends as scouts?

We regularly host scouts from AAA programs, USHL, OHL, and NCAA programs. Our network continues to grow each season.
